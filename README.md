# Infinite Inspiration ♾️

### 2024 CiGA GameJam Project (Topic: limited and infinite)

> The game was made within 48 hours


## 🎯 Overview
In this game, players step into the shoes of a developer participating in a GameJam. Your mission is to browse through a plethora of game ideas and collect inspiration. But time is limited! You'll have to make tough choices and prioritize which ideas to pursue. Will you create the next big hit or run out of time? 🕒💻

## 🌟 Features
- Browse and collect inspiration to fuel your creativity
- Manage your time wisely to complete your game
- Make difficult decisions about which ideas to pursue
- Experience the excitement and pressure of a real GameJam!

## 🚀 How to Play
1. Explore the available ideas and click to collect inspiration
2. Keep an eye on the clock ⏰
3. Decide which ideas to develop further
4. Complete your game before time runs out!
